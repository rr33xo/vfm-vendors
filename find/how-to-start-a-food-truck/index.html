Questions

Is VEYGO free for vendors?

The first 25 vendors get 3 months free with code BETA. After that it's $29 a month or $290 a year, cancel any time.

How do customers find my truck?

When you post your spot, your pin shows on VEYGO's live map with your hours and today's special. People nearby see you're open.

Do I need an app?

No. VEYGO works on the web on any phone, and there's an Android app.
