Questions

When and how am I paid?

Once you have $25 or more, you request a payout in your creator portal by Zelle, Venmo, PayPal or Cash App. Each request is reviewed, then sent.

What if a vendor cancels?

You earn your share of every payment they actually make in their first year. If they stop paying, there is nothing more to share.

Can I refer myself or my own business?

No. Self-referrals and fake sign-ups are not paid and the code is removed.
